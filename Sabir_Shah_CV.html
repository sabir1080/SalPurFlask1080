Key Project — Inventory & Double-Entry Accounting System

A complete, deployed trading and accounting web application. Every transaction posts into one double-entry general ledger, so stock, receivables, payables and the financial statements are always drawn from the same books.

  • Double-entry general ledger with control accounts, accounting periods and year-end closing; weighted-average inventory costing.
  • Point-of-Sale counter with barcode / QR scanning, multiple payment methods and thermal-receipt printing; printable barcode / QR item labels.
  • Fixed-asset register with straight-line and reducing-balance depreciation; gapless invoice numbering with yearly reset.
  • Financial reports — Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance, Cash Flow, and a Reconciliation report that proves the ledger agrees with its subledgers.
  • Immutable documents (reversed, never deleted) with a full audit trail; role-based access control; one-file backup & restore.
  • Configurable time zone, fiscal-year start and currency, so it runs correctly across different countries.
  • Built in Python / Flask / PostgreSQL; deployed to the cloud with continuous integration and 120+ automated tests.
