{# Language switch for the About page and the User Manual. Both languages are in the page and one is hidden, rather than fetched from the server. These are static documents — a round trip to swap the words a reader is already looking at would be slower and could fail. The choice is remembered, so a reader who picks Urdu on the manual does not meet English again on About. English is the default: it is what the software itself says, so the manual matches the buttons the reader is looking for. #}
