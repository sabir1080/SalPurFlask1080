{# Language switch for the About page and the User Manual. Both languages are in the page and one is hidden, rather than fetched from the server. These are static documents — a round trip to swap the words a reader is already looking at would be slower and could fail. Every visit opens in English, and the choice is deliberately *not* remembered. These two pages are the first thing a stranger sees — a prospective client, an employer — and they must not arrive in a language chosen once by someone else on this browser. The switch is right there for a reader who wants Urdu, and it costs them one click each time. #}
