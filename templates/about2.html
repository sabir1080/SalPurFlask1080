🌟 App Features
🛡️
Role-Based Access Control

Three levels — Admin, Manager, Staff. Each role sees only what they're allowed to. No code changes needed — admin controls it all from the UI.

👤
Web-Based User Management

Admin creates, edits and deletes users from a polished UI. Password confirmation prevents typos. Role changes take effect instantly.

🔐
Secure Authentication

Argon2 password hashing, email verification, CSRF protection on every form, and token-based password reset. Industry-standard security.

🏪
Supplier Ledger

Full supplier records with opening balance. Auto-generated ledger tracks every purchase, payment and return — with running balance.

👥
Customer Ledger

Complete customer records with opening balance. Every sale, receipt and return updates the ledger in real time. Know who owes what instantly.

📦
Inventory Management

Items organised by category. Purchase price, sale price and reorder level per item. Stock auto-adjusts on every purchase, sale or return.

🛒
Purchase Management

Record purchases with percentage or fixed discount and tax. Stock increases automatically. Print PDF invoices directly from the browser.

💰
Sale Management

Sales with discount and tax support. Insufficient-stock alert before saving. Stock decreases automatically. Printable sale invoices.

↩️
Returns System

Record purchase and sale returns against original bills. Stock and ledger are both corrected automatically — no manual adjustment needed.

💳
Payments & Receipts

Supplier payments and customer receipts via Cash, Bank, Cheque or Online. Link to a specific bill or leave unlinked — full flexibility.

📊
Dashboard & Analytics

Live KPIs, stock-level charts, purchase and sale trend graphs. Total payable, receivable and profit at a glance. (Manager+ only.)

📁
Reports & Exports

Date-range profit reports, item-wise and customer-wise breakdowns. Export to Excel (.xlsx) or CSV with a single click. (Manager+ only.)

📖 How to Use
1
Admin Creates the Team

First user is created via CLI (flask create-user) and gets Admin role. Admin then creates Manager and Staff users from Users → Create User in the web UI.

2
Sign In

Each team member signs in with their email and password. Their role determines exactly what they can see and do.

3
Set Up Categories & Items

Create product categories first, then add items with stock, purchase price, sale price and reorder level. Stock auto-updates thereafter. (Manager+ required.)

4
Add Suppliers & Customers

Add all parties with contact details and opening balances — the amount already owed before the system starts. (Manager+ required.)

5
Record Purchases

Select supplier, item, quantity and price. Apply discount and/or tax if needed. Stock and supplier ledger update automatically.

6
Record Sales

Select customer, item, quantity and price. Discount and tax can be applied. The app warns if stock is insufficient. Customer ledger updates automatically.

7
Handle Returns

Go to Purchase Returns or Sale Returns. Select the original bill and enter the returned quantity. Stock and ledger are both corrected automatically.

8
Record Payments & Receipts

Record supplier payments and customer receipts with their payment method. Link to a specific bill or leave open. Ledger updates instantly.

9
Print Invoices

Click the 🧾 icon on any purchase or sale row. A print-ready invoice opens in a new tab — press Ctrl+P to print or save as PDF.

10
Dashboard & Reports Manager+

Live KPI dashboard with charts. Reports page lets you filter by date, item or customer and export to Excel or CSV.
