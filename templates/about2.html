Built for reliability

The details that make it safe to run a real business on

Exact-decimal money
Balances are stored as fixed-point decimals — no floating-point rounding drift.
Complete audit trail
Every create, edit and delete is logged with who did it and when.
Role-based access
Sensitive actions are limited to the right roles; verified email required.
Secure by default
HTTPS, hashed passwords, CSRF protection, security headers and login throttling.
Portable backups
One-click JSON backup & restore that works on both SQLite and PostgreSQL.
Tested & monitored
An automated test suite guards the accounting core, with health checks and logging.
