Read the User Manual

One set of books

Inventory software usually keeps stock in one place and accounts in another, and quietly lets them disagree. This does not. Every purchase, sale, return, payment, expense and depreciation charge becomes a double-entry journal the moment it is saved. The Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance and Cash Flow Statement are all summed from those journals — not one of them is a separate calculation that can drift away from the others.

The subledgers — customer balances, supplier balances, stock on hand — are kept by their own documents, independently of the ledger. The Reconciliation report compares the two. If they agree, both are almost certainly right. If they ever differ, that is a real error and the system says so plainly.

The rules it will not let you break

An accounting system earns its keep by what it refuses to do.

A posted document is never edited or deleted — it is reversed or corrected. The original stays, marked reversed, with a cancelling journal entry beside it. Reversed invoices print with a REVERSED watermark, so a cancelled invoice can never be handed over as a live one. An admin can also correct a posted sale or purchase, and the reason is recorded.
Invoice numbers have no gaps. Numbers come from a counter that rolls back with the transaction, so a failed save never burns one. They restart each fiscal year, and a numbered document cannot be deleted — only reversed, keeping its number.
Stock can never go negative. Not by selling, not by adjusting, and not by reversing a purchase whose goods have already been sold — which is the case everybody forgets.
Control accounts belong to their subledgers. Receivables, Payables and Inventory cannot be touched by a manual journal entry. They move only when a document moves them, which is precisely why they always agree with the customer, supplier and stock ledgers.
Nothing posts into a closed period. Once a month or a year is closed, figures you have already reported cannot quietly change underneath you.

How you know the books are right

Four checks. Each compares two records kept independently of each other.

ReportWhat must be true
Balance Sheet Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Equity is not a balancing figure — it is added up on its own, so the two sides matching is a result, not an arrangement.
Trial Balance Total debits = total credits.
Reconciliation Receivables, Payables and Inventory in the ledger = the customer, supplier and stock ledgers underneath them.
Cash Flow Opening cash + movement = closing cash — and that closing figure equals the Cash & Bank page and the Balance Sheet.

Run them and they either all agree or they do not. There is no third answer, and no report here that can be made to look right on its own.

Worth knowing before you start
Stock is valued at weighted average.

Buy the same item at 100 and again at 140, and from then on every unit is costed at 120. Not FIFO, not LIFO. It is the same figure the Inventory account is posted, which is why the stock report and the balance sheet always agree.

Draft first, then post.

A purchase or sale is saved as a draft with no effect on stock or accounts. It takes effect when it is posted, and from then on it can only be reversed or corrected by an admin. POS sales, payments, receipts, returns and expenses take effect when saved.

One company, one currency.

The books of a single business, in a single currency. Multi-company and multi-currency are not supported.

Take backups.

Admin → Backup & Restore → Download Backup writes every table to one file. It is the only way back from a mistake and the only thing between you and a lost database. Make it a weekly habit.

Ek hi kitab

Agar dono muttafiq hain, to taqreeban yaqeeni tor par dono durust hain. Aur agar kabhi alag hon, to woh asli ghalti hai — aur system saaf saaf keh dega.

Jo usool ye tootne nahi deta

Accounting system ki asli qeemat is baat mein hai ke woh kya karne se inkaar karta hai.

Posted document na edit hota hai na delete — reverse ya correct hota hai. Asal document apni jagah rehta hai, uspar "reversed" ka nishaan lag jaata hai, aur saath ek ulti journal entry hoti hai. Reversed invoice par print mein REVERSED ka watermark aata hai — taake cancelled invoice kabhi asli bana kar na di ja sake. Admin post shuda sale ya purchase durust bhi kar sakta hai, aur wajah record hoti hai.
Invoice numbers mein koi gap nahi. Numbers ek aise counter se milte hain jo transaction ke saath hi wapas palat jaata hai — yaani nakaam save kisi number ko zaya nahi karta. Har fiscal year naye sire se shuru hote hain, aur numbered document delete nahi hota — sirf reverse, apna number rakhte hue.
Stock kabhi manfi nahi ho sakta. Na bechne se, na adjustment se, aur na us purchase ko reverse karne se jiska maal bik chuka ho — aur yehi woh surat hai jo har koi bhool jaata hai.
Control accounts apne subledgers ki milkiyat hain. Receivables, Payables aur Inventory par manual journal entry lag hi nahi sakti. Ye sirf tab hilte hain jab koi document inhe hilaye — aur bilkul isi liye ye hamesha customer, supplier aur stock ke ledger se milte hain.
Band period mein kuch post nahi hota. Jo mahina ya saal band ho gaya, uske woh numbers jo aap pehle hi report kar chuke hain, ab chupke se badal nahi sakte.

Kaise pata chale ke kitaben durust hain

Chaar check. Har ek do aise records ko milata hai jo ek doosre se bilkul azaad rakhe jaate hain.

ReportKya sach hona chahiye
Balance Sheet Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Equity yahan koi balancing figure nahi — woh alag se jama hoti hai. Isi liye dono taraf ka barabar hona nateeja hai, bandobast nahi.
Trial Balance Kul Debit = kul Credit.
Reconciliation Ledger ke Receivables, Payables aur Inventory = unke neeche walay customer, supplier aur stock ke ledger.
Cash Flow Shuru ka cash + harkat = aakhir ka cash — aur wohi aakhri number Cash & Bank page aur Balance Sheet ke barabar hona chahiye.

Inhe chalayein: ya to sab muttafiq honge, ya nahi. Teesra koi jawab hai hi nahi — aur yahan koi report aisi nahi jo akele apne bal par durust dikh sake.

Shuru karne se pehle ye jaan lein
Stock ki value weighted average par hai.

Ek hi item 100 ka khareedein aur phir 140 ka — ab har unit 120 par ginega. Ye FIFO nahi, LIFO bhi nahi. Yehi wohi raqam hai jo Inventory account mein post hoti hai — isi liye stock report aur Balance Sheet hamesha milte hain.

Pehle draft, phir post.

Purchase ya sale pehle draft ki surat mein save hoti hai jis ka stock ya accounts par koi asar nahi hota. Post karne par asar hota hai, aur uske baad sirf admin use reverse ya correct kar sakta hai. POS sales, payments, receipts, returns aur expenses save hote hi asar karte hain.

Ek company, ek currency.

Ye ek hi karobar ki kitaben ek hi currency mein rakhta hai. Multi-company aur multi-currency mein kaam nahi karta.

Backup lete rahein.

Admin → Backup & Restore → Download Backup poore database ki har table ek file mein likh deta hai. Ghalti se wapas aane ka yehi waahid raasta hai — aur database zaya hone se bachne ka bhi. Isay haftawar aadat bana lein.
