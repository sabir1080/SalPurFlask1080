Delete All Transactions

Keeps your suppliers, customers and items — they took real work to enter and are still correct. Deletes every purchase, sale, return, payment, receipt, expense, journal entry and fixed asset, and clears their opening balances and stock to zero. Invoice numbering starts again from 1.

For the start of a year, or when a trial period ends and real trading begins.

Delete All Data

Deletes everything — suppliers, customers, items and categories as well as all transactions. The system is left exactly as a new installation: chart of accounts, tax codes, this year's periods and the four cash/bank accounts, and nothing else.

For handing the system over to someone who will enter their own data.

Your user accounts are kept either way, so you stay signed in.
