📦 SalPurFlask

Sales & Purchase Management System — Simple, Fast & Reliable

🌟 App Features

🔐
Secure Login System

Secure signup and signin with email verification. Password reset is also done via email. Only verified users can access the app.

🏪
Supplier Management

Maintain complete records of all your suppliers — name, contact, and address. Search, add, edit, and delete with ease.

👥
Customer Management

Manage every customer's information — name, contact, and address — all in one place. Navigate records easily with pagination.

📦
Item / Inventory

Track item stock levels. Set separate purchase and sale prices. Define reorder levels so you're alerted when stock runs low.

🛒
Purchase Management

Record purchases from suppliers. Stock is automatically increased after each purchase. Track quantity, price, and date.

💰
Sale Management

Record sales to customers. Stock decreases automatically. An alert is shown if there is insufficient stock available.

📊
Dashboard & Charts

View stock levels, purchase trends, and sale trends via charts. See total purchase cost and total sale revenue at a glance.

📋
Reports & Profit

Generate date-wise, item-wise, and customer-wise profit reports. Export reports to CSV with a single click.

🔢
Smart Number Formatting

All amounts and quantities are displayed in 999,999,999,999.99 format — making large numbers easy to read.

📖 How to Use

1
Create an Account (Sign Up)

Click "Sign Up" in the navbar. Enter your name, email, and password. A verification link will be sent to your email — click it to activate your account.

2
Sign In

Sign in using your email and password. Login is only possible after email verification. Use "Forgot Password" if you forget your password.

3
Add Suppliers

Go to "Suppliers" from the navbar. Fill in the supplier's name, phone number, and address, then click "Add Supplier". Use the search bar to find any supplier quickly.

4
Add Customers

Go to the "Customers" page and fill in the customer details. Once added, you can select this customer when recording a sale.

5
Set Up Items / Inventory

Go to the "Items" page. For each item, set the name, initial stock, reorder level, purchase price, and sale price. Stock will be auto-updated by purchases and sales.

6
Record a Purchase

Go to "Purchases". Fill in the supplier, item, quantity, price, and date. After clicking "Add Purchase", the item stock increases automatically.

7
Record a Sale

Go to "Sales". Fill in the customer, item, quantity, price, and date. The app will alert you if stock is insufficient. Stock decreases automatically after the sale.

8
View the Dashboard

The Dashboard shows charts for stock levels, purchase trends, and sale trends. Total purchase cost and sale revenue are also displayed here.

9
Generate Reports

Go to "Reports", select a date range, and click "Generate Reports". View date-wise, item-wise, and customer-wise profit reports. Export to CSV is also available.
