Group accounts are headings: the balance shown against one is the total of the accounts beneath it. Control accounts are owned by their subledger — Accounts Receivable by the customer ledgers, Accounts Payable by the supplier ledgers, Inventory by stock movements — so no journal entry may be posted to them by hand. Every balance is shown on its natural side, so a positive number means what the account normally holds.
