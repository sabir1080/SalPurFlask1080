1. Before you begin

Three ideas explain almost everything about how this system behaves. Understanding them now will save you a great deal of confusion later.

Everything posts to one ledger

Every purchase, sale, return, payment, receipt, expense and depreciation charge is written into a double-entry general ledger at the moment you save it. Every report — Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance, Cash Flow — is added up from that ledger and nowhere else.

Why this matters to you If something has not been posted, it does not exist as far as the reports are concerned. This is the single most common source of "the numbers look wrong" — and the answer is almost always that a document was never saved, or was reversed.
Posted documents are reversed, never deleted

Once an invoice is saved it cannot be edited or deleted. If it is wrong, you reverse it: the original stays where it is, marked reversed, and the system posts a cancelling entry next to it. Both remain in the books for ever.

Why This is what an audit trail is. A system that lets you quietly delete an invoice you have already given to a customer is not keeping books — it is keeping a rough draft.
Stock is valued at weighted average

Buy 10 units at 100 and later 10 at 140, and you now hold 20 units worth 2,400 — an average of 120 each. Sell one and its cost is 120: not 100, not 140. Once goods enter the warehouse they lose their individual identity.

This is not FIFO and not LIFO. It is the same figure the Inventory account in the ledger is posted, which is exactly why the stock report and the balance sheet always agree.

2. First-time setup

Do these in order. Each step depends on the one before it.

  1. Check the chart of accounts. Accounting → Chart of Accounts
    It is created for you on first run. You may add your own expense accounts (Rent, Salaries, Utilities and so on). You cannot delete the accounts the system posts to.
  2. Check the fiscal year. Accounting → Periods
    The current calendar year and its twelve months are created for you. Nothing can be posted on a date that falls outside an open period.
  3. Set up your cash and bank accounts. Accounting → Cash & Bank Accounts
    Four are provided (Cash, Bank, Cheque, Online). Rename them or add your own — one per real bank account you hold.
  4. Enter your categories and items. Inventory → Categories, then Items
    Set a reorder level on each item so the system can warn you before you run out.
  5. Enter your suppliers and customers. Parties → Suppliers / Customers
  6. Enter opening balances — see the next section. Do this before you record a single transaction.

3. Opening balances

An opening balance is what you already owned or owed on the day you started using this system. There are four kinds, and each is entered in a different place.

WhatWhereMeaning
Cash / bankCash & Bank Accounts → editMoney already in the till or the bank.
SupplierParties → Suppliers → editWhat you already owed them.
CustomerParties → Customers → editWhat they already owed you.
StockInventory → Items → editGoods already on the shelf, and what they cost you.
These are real accounting entries, not just numbers on a form Each one is posted to the ledger against Opening Balance Equity. That is why they show up on the Balance Sheet, and why correcting one later does not double it — the system reverses the old entry and posts a fresh one.
Enter these first Enter opening balances before you record any trading. They can be corrected afterwards, but it is far easier to start from a true picture than to reconcile back to one.

When you are done, open the Balance Sheet. It should show exactly what you own and owe, and it must say Balanced.

4. Buying

Purchase invoice

Transactions → Purchase → New

Choose the supplier and the date, then add one line per item: quantity, price, and optionally a discount and a tax rate. Save.

What happens the moment you save:

  • Stock goes up, and the item's value goes up by what you actually paid.
  • The supplier's ledger shows a new payable.
  • A journal entry is posted: Dr Inventory, Cr Accounts Payable.
  • The invoice gets its number — PUR-2026-000001 — and that number is now permanent.
Purchase orders and quotations

Transactions → Purchase Orders / Quotations — these are not accounting documents. They move no stock and post nothing. They are intentions, and they become real only when converted into a purchase or a sale.

5. Selling

Transactions → Sale → New

Same shape as a purchase. On save:

  • Stock goes down. The cost of what left is the weighted average, and that becomes Cost of Goods Sold.
  • The customer's ledger shows a new receivable.
  • Two journal entries are posted: Dr Receivable, Cr Sales — and Dr Cost of Goods Sold, Cr Inventory.
  • The invoice is numbered INV-2026-000001.
Your profit is calculated at the moment of sale Not at month end, not by a report. The sale itself records what the goods cost, so the Profit & Loss is right the second the invoice is saved.

You cannot sell stock you do not have. The system will refuse and tell you how many are on hand.

6. Money in and out

Paying a supplier

Transactions → Supplier Payments — choose the supplier, the amount, and which cash or bank account the money came out of. You may link the payment to a specific bill, or leave it against the account generally.

Receiving from a customer

Transactions → Customer Receipts — the mirror image.

Expenses

Transactions → Expenses — rent, salaries, electricity. Choose an expense category; that is what decides which account it lands in on the Profit & Loss.

Always pick the right cash or bank account It is what makes the Cash & Bank page, the Cash Flow Statement and the Balance Sheet agree with your actual bank statement. Getting it wrong is not a display problem — the money is recorded as leaving the wrong place.

7. Returns

Transactions → Purchase Return — goods you send back to a supplier.
Transactions → Sale Return — goods a customer sends back to you.

A return is always made against the original invoice line, and you can never return more than was bought or sold on it.

A sale return comes back in at the cost it left at Not at today's average. If it went out costing 120 it comes back in at 120, even if the average has since moved. Anything else would invent or destroy profit that never existed.

Use a return when goods physically moved. Use a reversal (section 14) when the document itself was a mistake.

8. Inventory

Multiple units

Inventory → Items → Add / Edit — every item has one base unit (its Unit of Measure), which is the only unit stock is ever counted in. Below that, an Alternate Units table lets you add other units the item can be bought or sold in — a name, and how many base units it equals. A medicine's base unit might be Tablet, with Strip (= 10) and Box (= 100) as alternates; a cloth's base unit might be Meter, with Thaan as an alternate.

Once an item has alternate units, every line on a Purchase, Sale, Quotation, Purchase Order, and the POS counter shows a Unit dropdown. Buy in Box, sell in Strip — whichever unit is picked, the quantity and price on that line stay in that unit, but stock always moves in the base unit behind the scenes (quantity × factor). Purchase/Sale Returns are not asked for a unit — they always return in the same unit the original line was in.

An item with no alternate units behaves exactly as before There is nothing to set up for a single-unit item — it just has a base unit, same as always.
Stock Valuation

Reports → Stock Valuation — what is in the warehouse right now and what it cost. It takes no date range, because stock on hand is a snapshot, not a period.

This total is the Inventory line on the Balance Sheet The two are drawn from different places and must agree. If they ever do not, something is genuinely wrong.
Stock adjustments

Inventory → Stock Adjustment — for stock that changed without a purchase or a sale.

TypeDirectionWhen
Stock InInGoods found or received outside a purchase.
Count Correction (Increase)InA stocktake found more than the system said.
Count Correction (Decrease)OutA stocktake found less than the system said.
Damage Write-offOutGoods broken or spoiled.
Sample / Free IssueOutGiven away.
Stock OutOutAnything else that left.

Every adjustment is valued at the weighted average and posted to the ledger, so it shows in your profit. Choose the type honestly — a shortfall found at stocktake is a count correction, not damage.

Bulk import

Inventory → Bulk Import — add many Items, Customers or Suppliers at once from a CSV, Excel, or JSON file, instead of typing each one in by hand. Column headers must match the sample shown on the page exactly.

An imported item is treated exactly like one added by hand Category is required, prices cannot be negative, a barcode already used by another item is rejected, and the stock column becomes both the item's opening stock and its opening accounting value — so a bulk-imported item's cost and Stock Valuation are correct from the first sale, the same as an item you type in yourself.

A file is capped at 10,000 rows. Every import is logged with a success/failure count and, for any row that failed, the reason why — visible under Recent Imports on the same page.

9. Fixed assets

A fixed asset is something you bought to use, not to sell — a van, a computer, furniture. It is not an expense; it is an asset that wears out slowly, and depreciation is how that wear reaches your profit.

Adding an asset

Accounting → Fixed Assets → New Asset

  • Cost — what you paid.
  • Salvage value — what it will be worth when finished with. Often 0.
  • Method — Straight Line (the same amount every month) or Reducing Balance (a percentage of what is left).
  • Useful life — in months, for straight line.
  • Paid from — the cash or bank account the money left.

Posts Dr Fixed Assets, Cr Cash. Cash goes down; it is not an expense.

Running depreciation

Accounting → Fixed Assets → Run monthly depreciation — choose the month and post. Every eligible asset is charged in one entry: Dr Depreciation, Cr Accumulated Depreciation.

A month can only be posted once. Run it again and the system will tell you there is nothing to depreciate. Do it once a month, at month end.

Disposal

When you sell or scrap an asset, record the disposal and what you got for it. The system works out the gain or loss against its book value and posts it.

10. Point of Sale

Transactions → Point of Sale — a cash counter for fast, low-touch sales. It is especially useful if you ring up many small transactions in a day.

Starting a sale

Choose the customer (or leave it blank for "walk-in"), then type or scan an item code (barcode / QR). The first match appears. Add the quantity and press Enter. Repeat for each item. Discounts and taxes are applied per line, the same as in an invoice.

Payment and printing

When done, press Complete Sale. It creates a Sale invoice, numbered in sequence, posts to the ledger, and updates stock. Choose a cash or bank account. The receipt prints immediately.

Suspending a sale (Hold)

Press Hold to pause the current sale. It is held in memory and appears in the Held Bills list. You can resume it later by opening it and pressing Resume. A resumed sale keeps its customer, all items and their quantities, and the moment it is completed it is numbered and posted normally.

Hold is for interruptions If you are rung up halfway through a sale, hold it, serve another customer, and resume when you are free. The second customer's sale goes onto the counter separately.

Hold does not post. The moment you press Complete Sale — whether immediately or much later — a journal entry is posted, stock moves, and the invoice is numbered.

11. Delivery Challans

A delivery challan (or delivery note) is a transport document that moves with goods between warehouse and customer. It is not an invoice; it does not post and moves no stock. It is created before or alongside a sale to track what physically shipped.

Creating a challan

Transactions → Delivery Challans

Choose a sale or customer, enter the date, and add items with quantities. The challan is numbered automatically (independent of invoice numbers) and printed. It travels with the shipment as proof of what left the warehouse.

A challan is optional, but useful if goods are collected or shipped You might create a challan to give to the customer's driver, or to yourself as a reminder of what left the warehouse today. Some countries require them for tax or customs reasons.
Linking to a sale

A challan is not required to create a sale; the two are separate documents. But if you link a challan to a sale (by choosing that sale when creating the challan), the two appear together for reference. Changing one does not affect the other.

12. Barcodes & QR Codes

Every item can have a barcode or QR code for quick lookup and scanning. Use these to speed up the POS counter, reduce typing mistakes, and manage inventory.

Adding a barcode to an item

Inventory → Items → [edit item] — scroll to the Barcode / QR field and enter a code. Typically:

  • A barcode is a product code (like a UPC or EAN) printed on the box.
  • A QR code you generate yourself (the system can create a unique QR for the item's ID).

One item, one code. Save. The system will not let you use the same code twice.

Printing barcode labels

Inventory → Items → [select items] → Print Labels — the system generates a sheet of barcode stickers that print to a label printer. Stick them onto your shelves or products.

Scanning at the POS

At the POS counter, when prompted for an item, scan a barcode with a handheld scanner (a barcode gun). It types the code into the search box, and the item appears. Add quantity and press Enter. This is much faster than looking up item names.

13. Audit Log

Admin → Audit Log (admin only) — a permanent record of every change made in the system. It records who made the change, what they changed, when, and the detail of what changed.

What is recorded
  • Every purchase, sale, payment, receipt and expense created, edited, or reversed.
  • Every user login and every password change.
  • Every backup downloaded and every restore performed.
  • Every manual journal entry created or reversed.
  • Every item, supplier, customer, or account added or changed.
  • Every period or fiscal year closed.
  • Every user role change or account created / deleted.
Reading the log

The log lists actions in reverse chronological order (newest first). Click any row to see the full detail: the exact before-and-after values, the user who made the change, and the exact timestamp.

The audit log cannot be edited or deleted It is stored separately and is read-only. You cannot hide what you did, and nobody can hide what they did either. This is exactly what the accounting standard expects.

Export the audit log as CSV for your own records or for an auditor to review.

14. The general ledger

Manual journal entries

Accounting → Journal Entries → New Entry — for things no document covers: an owner putting money in, a bank charge, an accrual.

Debits must equal credits. The system will not accept an entry that does not balance.

Some accounts are missing from the dropdown, deliberately Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable and Inventory cannot be chosen. They are control accounts: they belong to the customer, supplier and stock ledgers, and they move only when a document moves them. If you could post to them by hand, the ledger and the customer's statement would stop agreeing and there would be no way to tell which was right.
Reversing a journal entry

Open it and press Reverse. A mirror entry is posted; both stay in the books. A reversal cannot itself be reversed, and an entry cannot be reversed twice.

Cash & bank account hierarchy

Admin → Financial Accounts (admin only) — group related cash or bank accounts under one heading. A Control Account (e.g. "Banks") is a header only; you never post to it directly. A Subsidiary Account (e.g. "HBL", "UBL") sits under a control account and is what actually appears in payment, receipt and POS dropdowns. A Standalone Account is not grouped at all.

Deactivating an account removes it from every selection dropdown without deleting its history; it still appears, greyed out, at the bottom of the Financial Accounts page, where it can be edited and reactivated at any time.

16. Month end

  1. Enter every invoice, payment and expense for the month.
  2. Run depreciation for the month — Accounting → Fixed Assets.
  3. Open the Reconciliation report. It must be green. If it is not, stop and find out why before doing anything else.
  4. Check the Balance Sheet says Balanced, and read the Profit & Loss.
  5. Download a backup.
  6. Optionally close the period (Accounting → Periods) so the month's figures can no longer change.

17. Year end

Do everything in the month-end list, then Accounting → Periods → Close Year.

Closing a year does two things:

  • Income and expense accounts are emptied into Retained Earnings, so the new year's Profit & Loss starts from zero.
  • The year is locked. Nothing more can be posted into it.
The Balance Sheet carries forward untouched That is the point of closing. Your profit stops being "this year's profit" and becomes part of the accumulated worth of the business.

Invoice numbering restarts at 1 for the new year: INV-2027-000001.

18. Fixing mistakes

You cannot edit or delete a posted document. You reverse it — open it and press Reverse — and then enter it again correctly.

Reversing undoes everything the document did:

  • a cancelling journal entry is posted;
  • stock goes back to where it was;
  • the customer's or supplier's ledger is corrected;
  • the document is marked Reversed and its invoice prints with a REVERSED watermark.
Some reversals are refused, and rightly You cannot reverse a purchase whose goods have already been sold — they are with the customer and cannot be handed back to the supplier. Reverse the sale first, or raise a purchase return instead. Likewise, an asset's purchase cannot be reversed once it has been depreciated; dispose of it instead.

Return or reverse? If the goods physically moved, it is a return. If the document should never have existed, it is a reversal.

20. Backup & restore

Admin → System

Download Backup writes every table in the database to a single file. Keep it somewhere that is not the same computer.

Restore replaces everything currently in the system with the contents of a backup file.

Take a backup every week, and before anything irreversible It is the only way back from a mistake. If the database is ever lost, an old backup is the difference between a bad afternoon and a destroyed business.

22. Rules & limits

What the system will not let you do
  • Edit or delete a posted document.
  • Sell, issue or write off stock you do not have.
  • Reverse a purchase whose goods have already been sold.
  • Post a journal entry that does not balance.
  • Post by hand to Receivables, Payables or Inventory.
  • Post into a closed period or a closed year.
  • Delete a numbered invoice — that would leave a gap in the sequence.
  • Depreciate the same month twice.
What it does not do
  • FIFO or LIFO costing — stock is weighted average.
  • Multiple currencies.
  • Multiple companies — one set of books per installation.
  • Budgets, cost centres, or recurring entries.

23. Troubleshooting

"The Profit & Loss shows nothing"

Check the date range. It defaults to 1 January until today, so anything dated in the future — or in a previous year — falls outside it.

"An expense I entered is not in the Profit & Loss"

Check it was not reversed, and check its date is inside the range you are looking at.

"The Balance Sheet does not balance"

This should be impossible. If it happens, run the Reconciliation report, take a backup, and report it — it means something is genuinely wrong, not merely displayed oddly.

"Reconciliation shows a difference"

The ledger and a subledger disagree. Do not post anything else. Take a backup and report it with a screenshot of that page.

"It says I cannot reverse this"

Read the message — it tells you why. Usually the goods have already been sold, or the document is already reversed. See section 14.

"Stock is right but the value looks wrong"

Remember it is a weighted average, not the last price you paid. Open Stock Valuation and compare the Avg Cost column with your purchases.

1. Shuru karne se pehle

Teen baaton se ye poora system samajh aa jaata hai. Inhe abhi samajh lena aage ki bohot si uljhan bacha lega.

Har cheez ek hi ledger mein jaati hai

Har purchase, sale, return, payment, receipt, expense aur depreciation — jis lamhe aap save karte hain, usi lamhe woh ek double-entry general ledger mein likh di jaati hai. Aur har report — Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Trial Balance, Cash Flow — usi ledger se jama hoti hai, kisi aur jagah se nahi.

Ye baat aap ke liye kyun ahem hai Agar koi cheez ledger mein post nahi hui, to reports ke nazdeek uska wajood hi nahi. "Numbers ghalat lag rahe hain" — is shikayat ki sab se aam wajah yehi hai, aur jawab taqreeban hamesha yehi nikalta hai ke document ya to save hi nahi hua tha, ya reverse ho chuka hai.
Posted document delete nahi hota — reverse hota hai

Ek bar invoice save ho gayi, to na woh edit ho sakti hai na delete. Agar ghalat hai to usay reverse karein: asal invoice apni jagah rehti hai, uspar "reversed" ka nishaan lag jaata hai, aur system uske saath ek ulti (cancelling) entry post kar deta hai. Dono hamesha ke liye kitabon mein rehti hain.

Kyun Audit trail ka matlab hi yehi hai. Jo system aap ko woh invoice chupke se mitane de jo aap customer ko de chuke hain — woh kitaben nahi rakh raha, kachi parchi rakh raha hai.
Stock ki value weighted average par hai

10 units 100 ke khareede, phir 10 units 140 ke. Ab aap ke paas 20 units hain jinki value 2,400 hai — yaani har unit 120 ka. Ek bechenge to uska cost 120 ginega: na 100, na 140. Godown mein aane ke baad maal apni alag pehchan kho deta hai.

Ye FIFO nahi hai, LIFO bhi nahi. Yehi wohi raqam hai jo ledger ke Inventory account mein post hoti hai — isi liye stock report aur Balance Sheet hamesha aapas mein milte hain.

    Har item par reorder level zaroor rakhein, taake maal khatam hone se pehle system aap ko aagah kar de.

Opening balance woh hai jo is system ko shuru karne ke din aap ke paas pehle se maujood tha, ya jiska aap par bojh tha. Iski chaar qismein hain, aur har ek alag jagah darj hoti hai.

4. Kharidari

Purchase invoice

Transactions → Purchase → New

Supplier aur tareekh chunein, phir har item ki ek line daalein: quantity, price, aur agar ho to discount aur tax. Save karein.

Save karte hi kya hota hai:

  • Stock barh jaata hai, aur item ki value utni barhti hai jitna aap ne asal mein ada kiya.
  • Supplier ke ledger mein naya payable aa jaata hai.
  • Journal entry post hoti hai: Dr Inventory, Cr Accounts Payable.
  • Invoice ko uska number milta hai — PUR-2026-000001 — aur ab woh number hamesha ke liye usi ka hai.
Purchase Orders aur Quotations

Transactions → Purchase Orders / Quotations — ye accounting documents nahi hain. Na stock hilate hain, na kuch post karte hain. Ye sirf iraade hain — asli tab bante hain jab inhe purchase ya sale mein tabdeel kiya jaye.

5. Farokht

Transactions → Sale → New

Shakal purchase jaisi hi hai. Save par:

  • Stock kam ho jaata hai. Jo maal gaya uska cost weighted average se ginta hai, aur wohi Cost of Goods Sold banta hai.
  • Customer ke ledger mein naya receivable aa jaata hai.
  • Do journal entries post hoti hain: Dr Receivable, Cr Sales — aur Dr Cost of Goods Sold, Cr Inventory.
  • Invoice ka number: INV-2026-000001.
Munafa usi lamhe nikal aata hai jab bikri hoti hai Na mahine ke aakhir mein, na kisi report se. Sale khud record kar leti hai ke maal kitne ka para tha — isi liye invoice save karte hi Profit & Loss durust ho jaata hai.

Jo maal aap ke paas hai hi nahi, woh bech nahi sakte. System inkaar kar dega aur bata dega ke stock mein kitna maujood hai.

6. Paisa aana aur jaana

Supplier ko adaigi

Transactions → Supplier Payments — supplier chunein, raqam likhein, aur ye zaroor batayein ke paisa kis cash ya bank account se nikla. Chahein to payment ko kisi khaas bill se joad dein, warna account ke against chhod dein.

Customer se wusooli

Transactions → Customer Receipts — bilkul ulti shakal.

Expenses

Transactions → Expenses — kiraya, tankhwah, bijli. Expense category chunein — usi se tay hota hai ke ye Profit & Loss mein kis account mein jayega.

Cash / Bank account hamesha durust chunein Yehi cheez Cash & Bank page, Cash Flow Statement aur Balance Sheet ko aap ke asli bank statement se milaati hai. Ghalat chunna sirf dikhawe ka masla nahi — paisa ghalat jagah se nikla hua record ho jaata hai.

7. Returns

Transactions → Purchase Return — jo maal aap supplier ko wapas bhejte hain.
Transactions → Sale Return — jo maal customer aap ko wapas karta hai.

Return hamesha asal invoice ki line ke against hota hai, aur us par jitna khareeda ya becha gaya tha, us se zyada wapas nahi ho sakta.

Sale return usi cost par wapas aata hai jis par gaya tha Aaj ke average par nahi. Agar 120 ke cost par gaya tha to 120 par hi wapas aayega, chahe average ab badal chuka ho. Warna aisa munafa banega ya khatam hoga jo kabhi tha hi nahi.

Return tab jab maal waqai hila ho. Reverse (section 14) tab jab document hi ghalat tha.

8. Inventory

Multiple Units (Ek se zyada units)

Inventory → Items → Add / Edit — har item ka ek base unit hota hai (uska Unit of Measure), jismein hamesha stock ginti hoti hai. Uske neeche Alternate Units wala table hota hai jahan aap us item ke doosre units add kar sakte hain jin mein wo khareeda ya becha ja sakta hai — ek naam, aur ye batana ke wo kitne base units ke barabar hai. Misaal: dawai ka base unit Tablet ho sakta hai, aur Strip (= 10) aur Box (= 100) uske alternate units; kapre ka base unit Meter ho sakta hai, aur Thaan uska alternate unit.

Jab item ke alternate units set ho jayein, to Purchase, Sale, Quotation, Purchase Order, aur POS counter — har jagah har line par ek Unit dropdown nazar aata hai. Box mein khareedein, Strip mein bechein — jo bhi unit chunein, us line ki quantity aur price usi unit mein rahegi, lekin stock hamesha base unit mein hi move hota hai (quantity × factor). Purchase/Sale Return mein unit poocha nahi jata — wo hamesha usi unit mein hota hai jis mein asal transaction hua tha.

Jis item ka koi alternate unit nahi, wo bilkul pehle jaisa hi chalta hai Single-unit item ke liye kuch alag se set up nahi karna — uska bas ek base unit hota hai, jaisa hamesha se tha.
Stock Valuation

Reports → Stock Valuation — abhi godown mein kya para hai aur woh kitne ka para hai. Ismein date range nahi hoti, kyunke stock ek snapshot hai, koi period nahi.

Iska total wohi hai jo Balance Sheet ki Inventory line hai Dono alag jagahon se aate hain, aur dono ka barabar hona lazmi hai. Agar kabhi alag hon, to koi cheez waqai kharab hai.
Stock Adjustment

Inventory → Stock Adjustment — us stock ke liye jo purchase ya sale ke baghair badla.

Bulk Import

Inventory → Bulk Import — Items, Customers ya Suppliers ek sath, bohat saari, CSV, Excel ya JSON file se add karein, ek ek karke type karne ki bajaye. Column headers page par diye gaye sample se hoo-ba-hoo match hone chahiye.

Ek file mein zyada se zyada 10,000 rows ho sakti hain. Har import log hoti hai — kitne successful, kitne failed, aur jo row fail hui uski wajah bhi — Recent Imports mein isi page par dikhti hai.

9. Fixed assets

Fixed asset woh cheez hai jo aap ne istemal ke liye khareedi, bechne ke liye nahi — gaari, computer, furniture. Ye kharcha nahi, asset hai jo ahista ahista ghista hai — aur depreciation wohi ghisawat hai jo aap ke munafe tak pahunchti hai.

Asset daalna

Accounting → Fixed Assets → New Asset

  • Cost — jitne ka khareeda.
  • Salvage value — istemal ke baad jitne ka rahega. Aksar 0.
  • Method — Straight Line (har mahine barabar raqam) ya Reducing Balance (jo bacha hai uska ek fisad).
  • Useful life — mahinon mein (straight line ke liye).
  • Paid from — jis cash ya bank account se paisa nikla.

Post hota hai: Dr Fixed Assets, Cr Cash. Cash kam hota hai — ye kharcha nahi.

Depreciation chalana

Accounting → Fixed Assets → Run monthly depreciation — mahina chunein aur post karein. Har qabil asset ek hi entry mein charge ho jaata hai: Dr Depreciation, Cr Accumulated Depreciation.

Ek mahina sirf ek bar post hota hai. Dobara chalayenge to system keh dega ke depreciate karne ko kuch nahi. Ye kaam mahine mein ek bar, mahine ke aakhir mein karein.

Disposal

Jab asset bech dein ya scrap kar dein, to disposal darj karein aur likhein ke kitne ka gaya. System uski book value ke muqable nafa ya nuqsan khud nikaal kar post kar deta hai.

10. Point of Sale

Transactions → Point of Sale — ek naqdaar counter jahan par din bhar chhote-chhote farosh din mein tak ho jaayein. Agar aap ko dhela dheli ke invoice din mein bahut karte hain to ye bikul sahi hai.

Sale shuru karna

Customer chunein (ya blank rakhen agar aaj ka customer nahi dikhta), phir item ka code likhen ya scan karein (barcode / QR). Pehla match aa jaata hai. Quantity daalen aur Enter daben. Har item ke liye ulta-pulta. Discounts aur taxes har line par lagti hain, bilkul invoice ki tarah.

Payment aur printing

Jab sab khatam, Complete Sale daben. Ye ek Sale invoice banati hai, number order se deti hai, ledger mein post karti hai, aur stock update karti hai. Cash ya bank account chunein. Receipt fauran print ho jaati hai.

Sale ko hold karna (Suspend)

Hold daben to abhi ka sale pause ho jaata hai. Wo memory mein rakhta hai aur Held Bills list mein dikhta hai. Baad mein usay doob sakte ho — usay kholo aur Resume daben. Resume shuda sale apna customer, sab items, quantities rakhta hai, aur jab usay complete karo to number aur posting normal tarah hoti hai.

Hold taqatul ke liye hai Agar ek customer ke beech mein doosra aajaye to hold kar do, doosre ko bilao, aur phir resume kar. Doosre customer ka sale counter par alag se jaata hai.

Hold post nahi karta. Jab aap Complete Sale daben — chahe fauran ya bahut baad mein — tab journal entry post hoti hai, stock hilta hai, aur invoice ko number milta hai.

11. Delivery Challans

Ek delivery challan (ya delivery note) ek transport document hai jo maal ke saath gaodaam se customer tak jaata hai. Ye invoice nahi; post nahi karta aur stock nahi hilata. Invoice ke pehle ya saath saath banaya jaata hai to track ho sake ke kya kya bheja.

Challan banana

Transactions → Delivery Challans

Ek sale ya customer chunein, tarikh daalen, aur items add karein quantities ke saath. Challan ko apne aap number mil jata hai (invoice number se alag) aur print ho jaata hai. Ye challan shipment ke saath jaata hai — proof kehne ke liye ke gaodaam se kya kya nikla.

Challan optional hai, lekin useful agar maal collect hota hai ya ship hota hai Aap challan banate ho customer ke driver ko dene ke liye, ya apne aap ke liye reminder ke liye — kal gaodaam se kya nikla. Kuch mulkon mein tax ya customs ke liye chahiye hota hai.
Sale se link karna

Challan sale ke liye zaruri nahi; dono alag hain. Lekin agar challan ko sale se link kro (challan banate waqt wo sale chunkar), to dono ek saath dikhte hain reference ke liye. Ek ko badal ne se doosra nahi badlta.

12. Barcodes aur QR Codes

Har item ke liye barcode ya QR code rakh sakte ho jaldi lookup aur scanning ke liye. Ye POS counter ko tez bna deta hai, typing ki ghalti kam hoti hai, aur inventory manage hona aasan hota hai.

Item mein barcode daalna

Inventory → Items → [item ko edit karein] — scroll karke Barcode / QR field tak jao aur code likho. Zyada tar:

  • Ek barcode product code hota hai (jaise UPC ya EAN) jo box par likha hota hai.
  • Ek QR code tum apne aap banate ho (system item ke liye apne aap unique QR bna sakta hai).

Ek item, ek code. Save karo. System dono ke liye ek hi code use nahi hone dega.

Barcode labels print karna

Inventory → Items → [items chunein] → Print Labels — system barcode stickers ka ek sheet banata hai jo label printer par print hote hain. Unhe shelves ya products par lagao.

POS mein scanning

POS counter par, jab item maanga jaye, barcode gun se scan kro. Ye search box mein code likhdeta hai, aur item aa jaata hai. Quantity do aur Enter daben. Ye item name likh ne se bahut tez hota hai.

14. General ledger

Manual journal entries

Accounting → Journal Entries → New Entry — un cheezon ke liye jinka koi document nahi: malik ka paisa daalna, bank charges, accrual.

Debit aur Credit barabar hone chahiyein. Jo entry balance nahi hoti, system usay leta hi nahi.

Kuch accounts dropdown mein jaan bujh kar nahi hain Accounts Receivable, Accounts Payable aur Inventory chune hi nahi ja sakte. Ye control accounts hain: inke maalik customer, supplier aur stock ke ledger hain, aur ye sirf tab hilte hain jab koi document inhe hilaye. Agar inpar haath se post kiya ja sakta, to ledger aur customer ka statement alag ho jaate — aur pata hi na chalta ke sach kaun keh raha hai.
Journal entry reverse karna

Entry kholein aur Reverse dabayein. Ulti entry post ho jaayegi; dono kitabon mein rahengi. Reversal khud reverse nahi ho sakti, aur ek entry do bar reverse nahi hoti.

Cash aur Bank Accounts ki Hierarchy

Admin → Financial Accounts (sirf admin) — mila-julay cash ya bank accounts ko ek heading ke neeche group karein. 18. Ghaltiyan theek karna

Posted document na edit hota hai na delete. Usay reverse karein — kholein aur Reverse dabayein — phir sahi tareeqe se dobara darj karein.

Reverse karne se document ka kiya hua sab kuch ult jaata hai:

  • ek ulti journal entry post hoti hai;
  • stock apni pehli haalat mein wapas chala jaata hai;
  • customer ya supplier ka ledger durust ho jaata hai;
  • document par Reversed ka nishaan lag jaata hai, aur uski invoice par REVERSED ka watermark chhapta hai.
Kuch reversals refuse ho jaati hain — aur theek hi hota hai Jis purchase ka maal bik chuka, woh reverse nahi ho sakti — woh maal customer ke paas hai, supplier ko wapas nahi kiya ja sakta. Pehle sale reverse karein, ya purchase return karein. Isi tarah jis asset ki depreciation chal chuki, uski kharid reverse nahi hoti — usay dispose karein.

Return ya Reverse? Agar maal waqai hila — to return. Agar document ka wajood hi ghalat tha — to reversal.

23. Masail ka hal

"Profit & Loss mein kuch nazar nahi aa raha"

Date range dekhein. Ye by default 1 January se aaj tak hoti hai — to jo cheez aage ki tareekh par hai, ya pichle saal ki hai, woh is arse se bahar reh jaati hai.

"Maine expense daala tha, Profit & Loss mein nahi hai"

Dekhein ke woh reverse to nahi hua, aur uski tareekh us arse ke andar hai jo aap dekh rahe hain.

"Balance Sheet balance nahi kar rahi"

Aisa hona hi nahi chahiye. Agar ho jaye to Reconciliation report chalayein, backup lein, aur ittila karein — iska matlab hai ke kuch waqai kharab hai, sirf dikhawe ka masla nahi.

"Reconciliation mein farq aa raha hai"

Ledger aur subledger alag ja rahe hain. Aur kuch post na karein. Backup lein aur us page ka screenshot bhej kar ittila karein.

"Keh raha hai reverse nahi kar sakte"

Paighaam parhein — wajah usi mein likhi hoti hai. Aksar maal bik chuka hota hai, ya document pehle se reversed hota hai. Section 14 dekhein.

"Stock theek hai magr value ajeeb lag rahi hai"

Yaad rakhein: ye weighted average hai, woh aakhri qeemat nahi jo aap ne ada ki. Stock Valuation kholein aur Avg Cost ka column apni kharidon se milaayein.
